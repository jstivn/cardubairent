Dubai Rental Guidelines

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers regarding documentation, security deposits, Salik toll fees, and delivery across Dubai.

What documents are required to rent a luxury car in Dubai?

For international visitors/tourists: Valid Passport, Tourist Entry Visa stamp, and your Home Country Driver’s License along with an International Driving Permit (IDP) if your country is not exempt under UAE RTA rules.

For UAE Residents: Valid Emirates ID, UAE Resident Visa copy, and valid UAE Driving License.

How does the security deposit work and when is it returned?

A refundable security deposit is pre-authorized by credit card or held in cash/crypto depending on the vehicle category. In compliance with RTA traffic fines clearance protocols, the deposit is released within 14 to 21 business days once all electronic Salik tolls and RTA cameras report clear records.

How are Salik tolls and daily mileage calculated?

All rentals include 250 km of daily mileage allowance across the UAE. Additional mileage is charged at a nominal per-km rate based on the vehicle model. Salik toll gates are automatically logged via the vehicle’s RTA transponder and settled transparently at 5 AED per gate.

Is insurance included in the quoted rental rate?

Yes. All vehicles carry 100% Comprehensive Casco Insurance coverage. A standard excess applies in the event of an at-fault accident accompanied by a police report. Premium zero-deductible CDW upgrade options are available upon client request.

Where can the car be delivered and handed over?

We provide complimentary direct delivery anywhere within Dubai: private villas, hotels (such as Burj Al Arab, Atlantis The Royal, Armani Hotel, Bulgari Resort), Dubai Marina yacht docks, or our Downtown office.

What is the minimum age requirement for sports cars and supercars?

The minimum age to rent luxury SUVs and sedans is 21 years with at least one year of driving experience. For high-performance supercars (Ferrari, Lamborghini, Rolls-Royce), the minimum age requirement is 25 years.

What payment methods are supported?

We accept Visa, MasterCard, American Express, direct bank wire transfer, cash (AED, USD, EUR, GBP), and cryptocurrency (USDT / Bitcoin) for both rental fees and security deposits.
